where it comes from, for example `Source 1, Article 3(1)`. If the sources do not

1. How does the directive define statutory interest for late payment?

   Answer: It is simple interest (not compound) at a rate equal to the reference rate
   plus at least eight percentage points (Source 1, Article 2(6)). For a Member State
   whose currency is the euro, the reference rate is either the interest rate the ECB
   applied to its most recent main refinancing operations, or the marginal rate from
   variable-rate tenders for those operations (Source 1, Article 2(7)(a)(i) and (ii)).
   The reference rate to use is the one in force on 1 January for the first half of the
   year and on 1 July for the second half (Source 1, Article 3(2)(a) and (b)).

2. The owner's note says the EU maximum late fee is 25 EUR. Is that right?

   Answer: No, the sources do not support it. The directive sets a fixed sum of EUR 40
   as a **minimum** for the creditor's recovery costs, not a maximum (Source 1, Article
   6(1)). The creditor can also claim reasonable compensation for recovery costs above
   that sum, for example lawyer or debt collection costs (Source 1, Article 6(3)). A
   term that excludes this compensation is presumed grossly unfair (Source 1, Article
   7(3)). The figure 25 EUR appears only in the owner's own note, which nobody has
   checked (Source 2, item 1 and its introduction); it is not in the directive extract.
   For this reason the messages in this tool do not mention any fee.

3. Does the business have to send a reminder before it is entitled to interest and to
   the fixed compensation sum?

   Answer: No. Between businesses the creditor is entitled to interest for late payment
   "without the necessity of a reminder" (Source 1, Article 3(1)), and the fixed sum is
   also payable "without the necessity of a reminder" (Source 1, Article 6(2)). The
   conditions are that the creditor has met its own contractual and legal obligations,
   and that the amount was not received on time unless the debtor is not responsible
   for the delay (Source 1, Article 3(1)(a) and (b)). Interest runs from the day after
   the payment date fixed in the contract (Source 1, Article 3(3)(a)). So the owner's
   belief (Source 2, item 2) that reminders are needed first is not what the extract
   says. Only Articles 1 to 7 are in the sources; whether national law or the contract
   adds a requirement is Not in the sources, and the owner would need to check it.

4. What is the current ECB reference rate?

   Answer: Not in the sources. The directive only says which rate counts: the ECB rate
   on its most recent main refinancing operations (Source 1, Article 2(7)(a)), fixed as
   the rate in force on 1 January or 1 July of the year (Source 1, Article 3(2)). No
   file gives an actual percentage. The owner asks for it in the final notice (Source 2,
   item 4). To answer, we would need the ECB's published rate for the relevant date from
   an official ECB source. The messages in this tool contain no rate.
